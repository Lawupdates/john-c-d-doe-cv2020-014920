Arizona Child Sexual Abuse Revival Law

The litigation included a constitutional challenge involving Arizona legislation permitting certain previously time-barred childhood sexual-abuse claims to proceed.

On August 26, 2021, the Arizona Superior Court issued a ruling addressing the challenge.

The case has subsequently been cited in legal materials concerning the constitutionality of civil revival laws for childhood sexual-abuse claims.

The Arizona Supreme Court denied review in CV-22-0003-PR on April 8, 2022.
