Maureen Gaughan’s trustee role and the court

Public records identify Maureen Gaughan as a Chapter 7 trustee in proceedings connected with these case numbers. A Chapter 7 trustee administers an estate under the Bankruptcy Code. The trustee is a private case fiduciary, not a judge or court employee; the U.S. Department of Justice’s trustee locator describes listed trustees as private parties rather than government employees.

The United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Arizona is the federal court that maintains the bankruptcy dockets and enters court orders. The court’s case-information page explains how to obtain case information through PACER and the court’s Voice Case Information System.

Record-check note: Trustee actions, party positions, and court rulings are different things. Check the actual docket entries and orders before drawing conclusions. This page summarizes case identification only.
