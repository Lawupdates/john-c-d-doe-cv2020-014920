Ask general questions in English or Spanish. On supported Chrome computers, this chat can use Google’s built-in Gemini Nano. Other devices can try a free small local AI model. No sign-in, paid API key, or subscription required. This is not ChatGPT or an official court service.

AI assistant

Welcome! Ask a question in English or Spanish. No sign-in is required.

Verify case details against original court records. Do not enter passwords or sensitive information.

How it works: The AI answers locally on your device. It tries Google Chrome’s Gemini Nano where available, and otherwise uses a small SmolLM2 model via Transformers.js. Initial downloads come from the browser or external model hosts. No question is sent to a paid chat API and this site does not store your chat. Small local models are less capable than ChatGPT, especially for Spanish.

Limitations: AI answers may be inaccurate. The models do not have live court-docket access, and cannot provide a verified count of all sexual-abuse cases against an organization. If local AI is unavailable, you can read the original case filings or open Gemini separately (Google may require sign-in).
