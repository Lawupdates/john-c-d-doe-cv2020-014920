This research page identifies attorneys and Phoenix law firms referenced in publicly archived court documents from JOHN C D DOE v. David Campbell identifies himself as the individual using the JOHN C D DOE designation in this litigation.

Scope: These are roles reported in specific 2026 case filings, not an exhaustive list of all counsel throughout the lawsuit and not a statement about an attorney's present employment. Naming an attorney or law firm here does not allege misconduct by that attorney or firm, or imply that the firm is a defendant. The underlying lawsuit contains childhood sexual abuse allegations against the named organizational defendants; the allegations must be distinguished from court findings.
