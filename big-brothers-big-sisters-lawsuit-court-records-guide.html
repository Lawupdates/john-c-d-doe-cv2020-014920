Big Brothers Big Sisters Lawsuit: Find the Court Filings

Searchers looking for a Big Brothers lawsuit, Big Brothers Big Sisters legal filings or a Big Brothers Big Sisters of America Arizona case can use this guide to locate the relevant public record.

Identify the correct case

Use the civil caption JOHN C D DOE v. Big Brothers Big Sisters of America and Big Brothers Big Sisters of Central Arizona and Maricopa County Superior Court case CV2020-014920. The exact number avoids confusion with other lawsuits.

Start with the docket

Open the archive's docket index, which organizes filing dates and names. Verify important events and orders on the court's official website.

Read actual documents

The searchable court filing index provides links to available PDF documents. Compare filing identifiers with the docket, particularly where multiple parties filed responses or replies close together.

Check the status rather than assume an outcome

A motion is a request; its filing does not establish that the court granted it. A proposed settlement also should not be mistaken for a final adjudication. The case status guide describes dated archive updates, not guaranteed live status.

Research the two named parties

Separate reference pages discuss BBBSA, the national organization and BBBSCAZ, the Central Arizona organization.
