What is the Big Brothers Big Sisters sexual abuse lawsuit?

This public-record guide concerns an Arizona civil lawsuit alleging childhood sexual abuse connected to a youth mentoring placement. The named defendants are Big Brothers Big Sisters of America (BBBSA) and Big Brothers Big Sisters of Central Arizona (BBBSCAZ). The allegations are claims in litigation, not findings of liability.
