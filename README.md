# JOHN C D DOE v. Big Brothers Big Sisters — Arizona Public Court Record Archive

**Case:** *JOHN C D DOE v. Big Brothers Big Sisters of America and Big Brothers Big Sisters of Central Arizona*  
**Court:** Superior Court of Arizona in Maricopa County  
**Case number:** CV2020-014920

[Open the independent case archive](https://lawupdates.github.io/john-c-d-doe-cv2020-014920/)

This repository supports an independent archive of publicly available filings in an Arizona civil case involving claims of childhood sexual abuse and Big Brothers Big Sisters organizations. The archive organizes the records by filing and provides links to the official court docket and other source materials.

## Scope and accuracy

This is an independent public-record archive, not an official court website and not legal advice. Claims in court filings are allegations unless and until established by a court. The official docket is the source to check for current case status and court rulings. The archive may not reflect later docket entries.
