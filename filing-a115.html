SUPERIOR COURT OF ARIZONA
MARICOPA COUNTY
CV 2020-014920 — 10/27/2026
HONORABLE CHRISTOPHER WHITTEN
MINUTE ENTRY

The Court has considered John C D Doe’s Limited Motion to Correct the Record, Vacate Settlement-Based Inactive Status, Restore Case to Active Calendar, and Reinstate Pretrial/Trial-Setting Conference, filed October 5, 2026, along with Defendants’ Response and Motion to Strike, filed October 13, 2026, Doe’s Response, filed October 14, 2026, and Defendants’ Reply, filed October 22, 2026.

First, Doe is no longer a party to this litigation. In May 2025, Doe expressly acknowledged, through independent counsel, that the claims asserted in this action belong to the bankruptcy estate and are controlled by the Chapter 7 Trustee. In September 2025, the Court approved the substitution of Trustee Maureen Gaughan as Plaintiff. Doe has not sought intervention under Rule 24, Ariz. R. Civ. P., or otherwise established authority to seek affirmative relief in this action. His motion is therefore unauthorized and subject to being stricken under Rule 7.1(f), Ariz. R. Civ. P.

Second, even if the Court were to consider the motion on its merits, Doe lacks standing to obtain the requested relief. The Trustee, not Doe, controls the claims asserted in this litigation, including decisions concerning their settlement and disposition. Doe's disagreement with the reported settlement does not authorize him to direct the procedural course of this action.

IT IS ORDERED granting Defendants' Motion to Strike pursuant to Rule 7.1(f), Ariz. R. Civ. P.

IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that, alternatively and independently, Doe's October 5, 2026 motion is denied for lack of standing.

IT IS FURTHER ORDERED denying Defendants' requests for attorneys' fees and sanctions under Rule 11, Ariz. R. Civ. P., and A.R.S. § 12-349.
