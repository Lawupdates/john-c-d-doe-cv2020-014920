BBBSCAZ — Big Brothers Big Sisters of Central Arizona in Court

This research page identifies Big Brothers Big Sisters of Central Arizona, also searched as BBBSCAZ or BBBSAZ, as a named defendant in the Arizona civil lawsuit JOHN C D DOE v. Big Brothers Big Sisters of America and Big Brothers Big Sisters of Central Arizona, case CV2020-014920.

Local and national parties

The Central Arizona organization and the national Big Brothers Big Sisters of America organization are separately named parties. Readers should distinguish each party's pleadings and arguments when reviewing the record.

Finding the official filings

Use the court docket index to identify dates and filing entries. The searchable PDF filing archive allows readers to inspect available documents directly. A party's allegation does not establish a judicial finding.

How to follow the proceedings

The dated case status guide summarizes archived activity. For authoritative current information check the Maricopa County Superior Court directly.

Source integrity

When reporting on litigation, identify the specific docket entry, filing date and court order. Avoid treating contested assertions, proposed agreements or individual motions as findings of liability.
