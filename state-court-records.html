How to look up a civil case by number or name

For an example of the steps in practice, use the Arizona civil court records guide for case CV2020-014920. It links the court docket to the archive’s copies of the complaint, answers and orders. If a docket lists a filing without the document, see how to find and request Arizona civil court filings. For other jurisdictions, see how to look up a sexual abuse lawsuit.

  1. Identify the jurisdiction. Look for the court name on the first page of a filing. If you only have a party name, also look for the county and the year the case began.
  2. Enter the exact case number first. If the portal uses a different punctuation format, follow its examples. If searching by party name, check the case caption before assuming a result is the one you want.
  3. Match the docket entry to the document. Record the date, filing title and entry number when available. If the public portal only shows an entry, ask the clerk how to request the document.

Availability varies. A failed search can mean the records are kept in a different court, the case is indexed differently, or access is restricted.

Worked example. Open the Maricopa County docket for CV2020-014920, then compare it with the John C D Doe filing index and the October 2026 minute entry.
